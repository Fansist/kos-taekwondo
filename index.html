What the school teaches

Traditional Korean Taekwondo for kids and adults, martial arts classes for children in a structured setting, and self-defense training.

To ask about a class, call (619) 690-5408.

Character and discipline

Alongside Taekwondo and self-defense, the school teaches a character and discipline curriculum:

  • Cooperation
  • Goal-setting
  • Focus
  • Confidence

One school, two names

  1. Founded asLee's Taekwondo
  2. Since 2013Ko's Taekwondo

The school opened as Lee's Taekwondo. In 2013 it was passed on and became Ko's Taekwondo, carrying forward its long-running, discipline-focused teaching. It is a small, no-frills neighborhood family dojang.

Families who review the school say its classes build self-defense skills, discipline and patience in children, in a structured class setting.
