Our Dojang

Small school. Long legacy.

Ko's Taekwondo is a neighborhood Korean martial arts dojang teaching traditional Taekwondo to kids and adults. The school was founded under prior ownership and passed to Master Ko in 2013, carrying forward a long-running local legacy of discipline-focused instruction.

“Families highlight that the school builds self-defense skills, discipline, and patience in children in a structured class setting.”

Classes run in a straightforward, no-frills format focused on the fundamentals of the art, no gimmicks, just steady progress on the mat.

Good to Know

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What age groups do you teach?
Ko's Taekwondo teaches both kids and adults, with classes structured to build discipline, focus, and self-defense skills for younger students in particular.
What will my child learn?
Classes emphasize a character-based curriculum alongside Taekwondo technique, including cooperation, goal-setting, focus, and confidence, in a structured class setting.
What are your class hours?
The dojang is open weekdays from 3:30pm, 8:30pm and Saturdays from 9:30am, 12:30pm. Closed Sundays.
Do you have a website or online booking?
No independent website is available. The best way to reach Ko's Taekwondo is by phone or by stopping by the dojang in person during class hours.
Who teaches the classes?
The school has been run by Master Ko since 2013.
